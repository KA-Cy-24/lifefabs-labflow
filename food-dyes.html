05 · Physics, units & limitations

Electric field & migration

E = V / Lelectrodes (V/m). Anionic dyes drift toward the positive electrode:

v = |μgel| E
x(t) = xwell + v t

μgel = μ0 exp(−k c s), with agarose c in % w/v and editable sieving multiplier s. This exponential attenuation is a heuristic, not a validated dye–agarose law.

Diffusion & band width

A Gaussian band uses σ²(t) = σ₀² + 2 D t, with illustrative initial σ₀ = 1.0 mm. For a dilute free-ion approximation D ≈ |μgel| kBT / (|z|e) (Einstein relation); this relation ignores gel-specific diffusion hindrance beyond the assumed mobility factor.

What this does not predict

Real bands depend on buffer and pH, ionic strength, heat, electro-osmosis, gel microstructure, staining intensity, batch impurities, interactions, and apparatus geometry. Nominal E-number charge and molecular mass are not enough to derive mobility. Band distances here are scenario predictions only; do not use them to design live high-voltage equipment or claim experimentally verified separations.
